# Mulenge Scholars' Network Canada (MSNC)

A community-driven platform supporting and uplifting Mulenge youth in Canada and around the world through mentorship, academic guidance, and leadership development.

## About MSNC

Mulenge Scholars' Network Canada (MSNC) was established in response to the growing academic, professional, and social needs of youth from the Mulenge community living in Canada. MSNC provides a platform where youth can learn from one another, access mentorship, build academic and professional skills, and strengthen their sense of identity and belonging.

## Vision

To build a united and empowered community where Mulenge youth discover their potential, thrive academically and professionally, and grow into leaders who contribute meaningfully to society.

## Mission

To support and uplift Mulenge youth in Canada and around the world through mentorship, academic guidance, and leadership development, while fostering partnerships with organizations that share common goals of education, empowerment, and community advancement.
